Important Alerts

Key reminders
!

⚠️ Do Not Travel Without Advance Parole

After filing I-485, leaving the U.S. without Advance Parole automatically abandons your green card application. Confirm with an attorney before any travel.

!

⚠️ Maintain Valid F-1 Status

Stay enrolled full-time, report address changes to DSO within 10 days, and keep your I-20 valid until I-485 is approved or EAD received.

i

RFE Response Window: 87 Days

If USCIS issues a Request for Evidence, you have 87 days from the RFE notice date to respond. Do not miss this deadline.

i

Priority Date: Currently Current

EB-2 Bangladesh (Rest of World) is Current as of May 2026. Check travel.state.gov Visa Bulletin monthly to confirm it stays current.

i

Medical Exam Valid for 2 Years

Form I-693 is valid for 2 years from the date the civil surgeon signs it. Do not get the exam too early — it must still be valid when USCIS adjudicates your I-485.

i

Keep Copies of Everything

Keep a complete copy of your filed I-140 package. If USCIS loses documents, you will need to resubmit. Store a digital copy in cloud storage.
